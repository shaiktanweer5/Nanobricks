Nanobricks Client Case Study

IT Standardization for SG Testing

How Nanobricks helped SG Testing, a calibration services business in Hyderabad, improve its office IT environment through endpoint standardization, local networking, controlled shared access and practical security improvements.

Request IT Assessment Explore Managed IT Services

Project Overview

A practical business IT standardization project for SG Testing in Hyderabad.

Client

SG Testing

Industry

Calibration Services

Environment

Approximately 10 business computers

Location

Hyderabad, Telangana

The Challenge

SG Testing relied on multiple office computers for day-to-day business activities including documents, spreadsheets, PDF files, printing and shared business data.

The environment required better consistency across devices, user access, software, security settings and local network usage.

Key requirements

  • Standardize office computer configurations.
  • Improve user and device organization.
  • Configure local network access between systems.
  • Provide controlled access to shared business data.
  • Enable local printer access where required.
  • Improve endpoint security and update practices.
  • Identify older systems requiring replacement.

What Nanobricks Implemented

The office environment was standardized around a practical baseline designed for everyday business use and easier support.

Device Naming

Business computers were organized using a consistent naming convention to simplify identification, administration and troubleshooting.

Windows 11 Pro Baseline

Supported business systems were standardized around Windows 11 Pro with common configuration and security settings.

Endpoint Security

Microsoft Defender, Windows Firewall and regular operating-system updates formed part of the endpoint baseline.

User Access

User accounts and password controls were configured to reduce unnecessary shared system access.

USB Controls

Removable-media restrictions were applied where required to reduce unnecessary data movement through USB devices.

Business Applications

Common office applications, browsers, PDF software and printer requirements were standardized across systems.

Printer Sharing

Local printer connectivity and sharing were configured so authorized systems could access required office printers.

Hardware Planning

Older or unsuitable computers were identified so replacements could be prioritized based on practical business requirements.

Local Network & Shared Access

The project also included practical local-network configuration to support shared business data and office devices.

Office Network Local LAN connectivity
Business PCs Approximately 10 systems
Shared Data & Printers Permission-based access

Shared Business Data

Shared folders were configured across the local network based on business requirements. Access was assigned according to the user's role, including appropriate read or edit permissions.

Local Device Access

Network connectivity between office systems was configured and tested to support shared resources including business folders and locally connected printers.

Security Without Unnecessary Complexity

SG Testing needed practical controls that could be maintained easily in a small-business environment.

Nanobricks focused on creating a consistent baseline instead of introducing unnecessary enterprise complexity.

  • Windows 11 Pro baseline.
  • Microsoft Defender protection.
  • Windows update standardization.
  • Controlled user access.
  • Permission-based shared folders.
  • USB restrictions where required.
  • Local network and printer access.
  • Standard business applications.

Business Outcome

SG Testing now has a more structured, consistent and support-ready office IT environment.

  • More consistent workstation configurations.
  • Easier identification of office computers.
  • Controlled access to shared business data.
  • Improved endpoint-security baseline.
  • Reliable local printer and network access.
  • Clearer hardware replacement priorities.
  • A repeatable IT standard for future systems.

A Practical Small-Business IT Approach

Good business IT does not always require complex infrastructure. Standardized devices, controlled access, reliable local networking and a clear security baseline can significantly improve day-to-day IT operations.
