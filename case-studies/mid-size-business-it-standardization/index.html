Mid-Size Business IT Standardization

A practical approach for growing organizations that need stronger consistency across endpoints, users, networking, security, cloud services, backups and ongoing IT operations.

When Businesses Outgrow Basic IT

As organizations grow, informal IT practices become harder to manage and support consistently.

More Endpoints

Growing numbers of desktops, laptops and business devices.

More Users

Multiple teams, departments and access requirements.

More Services

Cloud, collaboration, storage and business applications.

More Risk

Greater security, access and business-continuity requirements.

Standardization Areas

Nanobricks can help build a consistent IT baseline across the main layers of a growing business environment.

Endpoint Standardization

Standard device naming, operating-system baselines, security settings, business applications and update practices.

Identity & Access

User accounts, administrator controls, role-based access and appropriate permissions for business information.

Network Standardization

Structured local networking, shared resources, device connectivity and controlled access between systems.

Endpoint Security

Endpoint protection, firewall controls, security updates and practical hardening based on business requirements.

Cloud & Collaboration

Structured use of cloud storage, email, collaboration and business applications with appropriate access controls.

Backup & Continuity

Identify critical business information and define appropriate backup, recovery and continuity practices.

Business Connectivity

Review office connectivity, shared devices, printers and network-dependent business workflows.

IT Documentation

Maintain practical records for devices, software, responsibilities, access requirements and support processes.

Practical IT Architecture

Standardization creates a clearer operating model instead of a collection of independently managed devices.

Built Around Business Requirements

Mid-size businesses do not need unnecessary complexity. They need an IT environment that can grow without becoming difficult to manage.

Nanobricks focuses on practical improvements based on the organization's users, devices, data and operational needs.

  • Standard device configurations.
  • Clear identity and access controls.
  • Structured networking.
  • Practical security controls.
  • Cloud and collaboration readiness.
  • Backup and recovery planning.
  • Hardware lifecycle planning.
  • Support-ready documentation.
