About Nanobricks

Nanobricks is a Hyderabad-based technology services company helping small and growing businesses build, secure, modernize and manage their technology environments.

Our services span Managed IT, Business IT Setup, Cloud Services, Cloud Security, Cybersecurity, DevOps & Automation, Backup & Continuity, Web & Software Solutions and IT Consulting.

Nanobricks also develops practical technology tools designed to help businesses and users understand, assess and work with technology more effectively.
