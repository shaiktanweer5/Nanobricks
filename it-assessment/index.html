What Can Be Included in the Assessment?

The assessment can be tailored to your environment. You do not need to have every technology listed below.

Devices & Endpoints

PCs, laptops, operating systems, device configuration, lifecycle and endpoint readiness.

Security

Endpoint protection, access controls, configuration risks and security requirements.

Business Email

Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, mailbox requirements and business productivity.

Cloud

AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, cloud adoption and operational requirements.

Backup & Recovery

Backup coverage, storage, restore readiness and continuity requirements.

Network & Wi-Fi

Business connectivity, Wi-Fi, shared resources and network requirements.

Office Technology

Printers, scanners, shared storage and practical office technology requirements.

Digital Systems

Websites, software, automation, portals and modernization opportunities.

How the Assessment Works

A simple process designed to understand the requirement before recommending technology.

01

Share

Tell us about your business, users, devices and current IT challenges.

02

Understand

Nanobricks reviews the requirement and identifies relevant areas to assess.

03

Review

The current environment can be reviewed remotely or onsite depending on scope.

04

Recommend

Practical improvements and next steps can then be discussed with the business.
