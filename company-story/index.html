Why Nanobricks Exists

Technology environments often grow faster than the processes used to manage them. Businesses may have cloud services, employee laptops, business email, backups, websites, applications and security tools, but limited visibility into whether everything is configured, protected and maintained properly.

Nanobricks was created to help bridge that gap with practical technology services, assessments and tools designed around real operational needs.

Our approach is simple: understand the problem first, assess the existing environment, identify practical improvements and implement solutions that align with the business instead of adding unnecessary complexity.

PRACTICAL TOOLS

From Services to Practical Technology Tools

Nanobricks also develops practical tools that help organizations understand their technology environments.

Our first public utility, Nanobricks Windows Endpoint Assessment V3.0.1, provides a simple PASS / REVIEW / FAIL view of key Windows endpoint configuration and security areas.

The tool reviews Windows version, RAM, disk capacity, Microsoft Defender, Windows Firewall, pending Windows Updates, Microsoft Office, local users and administrator accounts.

The goal is not to replace enterprise security platforms. It is designed to give businesses and IT teams a simple starting point for understanding endpoint readiness and identifying areas that may require attention.
