What Is IT AMC?

An IT Annual Maintenance Contract provides ongoing support, monitoring and maintenance for your organization's technology environment.

For Businesses Without a Dedicated IT Team

Nanobricks can act as your extended IT support team for routine technology operations, troubleshooting and maintenance.

For Businesses With Internal IT Teams

We can support your existing team with cloud operations, patch coordination, user administration, backups and ongoing maintenance.

Managed IT & AMC Services

Support packages can be customized based on users, devices, infrastructure and business requirements.

Endpoint Support

Support for laptops, desktops, operating systems, common applications and user issues.

Patch Management

Planned operating system and application update coordination, including maintenance windows and approval workflows.

Server Support

Routine administration, monitoring, maintenance and operational support for business servers.

Cloud Operations

AWS, Azure and GCP administration, monitoring, backup coordination and routine cloud operations.

Microsoft 365 & Google Workspace

User administration, email configuration, onboarding, offboarding and security configuration.

How Our AMC Engagement Works

1. Assessment

2. Scope

3. Support Plan

A monthly or annual support plan is created based on your environment.

4. Ongoing Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you support small businesses?

Yes. Managed IT services are particularly suitable for growing businesses that do not maintain a full internal IT team.

Do you provide 24/7 support?

Support hours and response requirements depend on the agreed service scope. They are defined clearly before the AMC begins.

Can you manage Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?

Yes. We can support user administration, onboarding, offboarding, email configuration and security settings.

Can you support AWS, Azure and Google Cloud?

Yes. Cloud administration and operational support can be included depending on the environment and agreed scope.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Yes. Website AMC can include minor updates, backups, SSL monitoring and domain-related support.

How is AMC pricing decided?

Pricing depends on the number of users, devices, servers, cloud resources, applications and required support scope.
