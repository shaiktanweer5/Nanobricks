Privacy & Data

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nanobricks may collect, use and handle information when you visit nanobricks.in, submit an enquiry or communicate with us.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. About Nanobricks

Nanobricks is a technology services business based in Hyderabad, India, providing services such as Managed IT, Cloud Services, Cloud Security, Cybersecurity, DevOps, Software Solutions and professional technology training through Nanobricks Academy.

2. Information We May Collect

When you use our website or contact Nanobricks, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Company, college or organization name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service or program you are interested in
  • Preferred contact method
  • Information included in your enquiry or requirement
  • Other information you choose to provide to us

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted to Nanobricks may be used for legitimate business purposes including:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests
  • Understanding your technology or training requirements
  • Preparing proposals, quotations or service recommendations
  • Communicating regarding Nanobricks services or Academy programs
  • Providing customer or participant support
  • Improving our website, services and customer experience
  • Maintaining business and enquiry records
  • Meeting applicable legal or regulatory obligations

4. Website Enquiry Forms

When you submit an enquiry through nanobricks.in, the information you provide may be recorded in Nanobricks business systems and used to contact you regarding your enquiry.

Please avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials, access keys, authentication secrets or other highly sensitive information through our general website enquiry form.

5. Analytics and Website Usage

Nanobricks may use website analytics technologies such as Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website.

Analytics services may collect information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, pages visited, referral source, interaction events and similar technical information.

This information is generally used to understand website performance and improve the experience of visitors.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Nanobricks and services used by the website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, analytics and performance measurement.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

7. How Information May Be Stored

Information submitted through Nanobricks may be stored using business productivity, email, cloud or record-management systems used to manage enquiries and customer communications.

We aim to take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

8. Third-Party Services

The Nanobricks website may rely on third-party technology providers for services such as website hosting, cloud infrastructure, analytics, email, forms and communication.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

9. WhatsApp, Email and External Links

Our website may include links allowing you to contact Nanobricks through WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn or other third-party platforms.

When you use those services, information may also be handled according to the privacy practices of the relevant platform.

Nanobricks is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services that are outside our control.

10. Sharing of Information

Nanobricks does not intend to sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers or professional advisers when reasonably necessary to operate the business, provide requested services, maintain the website or comply with applicable legal obligations.

11. Data Retention

Nanobricks may retain enquiry, customer, training and business communication information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, ongoing business relationships, record keeping or applicable legal requirements.

Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

12. Information Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information handled by Nanobricks.

However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Your Requests

If you have submitted personal information to Nanobricks, you may contact us regarding questions, corrections or reasonable requests relating to that information.

We may need to verify the identity of the requester before acting on certain requests.

14. Children's Information

Nanobricks Academy may offer educational programs that can involve students. Where participation by minors is permitted, additional information or appropriate consent from a parent, guardian, institution or authorized representative may be requested where required.

The general Nanobricks website is not intended to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Nanobricks may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, technology systems or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page together with the updated revision date.

This Privacy Policy is intended to provide general information about Nanobricks' current website and business practices. Specific customer projects, employment arrangements, training programs or commercial engagements may be subject to additional agreements, notices or terms.
