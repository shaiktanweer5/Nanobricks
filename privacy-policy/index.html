Privacy & Data

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nanobricks may collect, use, store and handle information when you visit nanobricks.in, submit an enquiry or communicate with us.

Last Updated: November 2026

1. About Nanobricks

Nanobricks is a technology services business based in Hyderabad, India, providing services including Managed IT, Business IT, Cloud Services, Cloud Security, Cybersecurity, DevOps, Software Solutions, IT Consulting and professional technology learning through Nanobricks Academy.

2. Information We May Collect

When you use our website or contact Nanobricks, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Company, college or organization name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service or program you are interested in
  • Preferred contact method
  • Information included in your enquiry or requirement
  • Other information you choose to provide

3. How We May Use Information

Information submitted to Nanobricks may be used for legitimate business purposes including:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests
  • Understanding technology or training requirements
  • Preparing proposals, quotations or recommendations
  • Communicating regarding services or Academy programs
  • Providing customer or participant support
  • Improving our website, services and customer experience
  • Maintaining appropriate business records
  • Meeting applicable legal or regulatory obligations

4. Website Enquiry Forms

Information submitted through nanobricks.in may be recorded in business systems used by Nanobricks and used to respond to your enquiry or requirement.

Please do not submit passwords, authentication secrets, access keys, banking credentials or other highly sensitive information through a general website enquiry form.

5. Analytics and Website Usage

Nanobricks may use analytics technologies such as Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website and to improve website performance.

Analytics services may process technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, pages visited, referral source and interaction events.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Nanobricks and third-party services used by the website may use cookies or similar technologies for website functionality, analytics and performance measurement.

You can control or delete cookies using your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

7. Storage and Security

Information submitted to Nanobricks may be stored using email, cloud, productivity or record-management systems used to manage enquiries, customers and communications.

We aim to use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

Nanobricks may use third-party providers for website hosting, cloud infrastructure, analytics, email, communication, productivity and related business services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. WhatsApp, Email and External Platforms

Our website may include links allowing you to contact Nanobricks through WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn or other third-party platforms.

When you use those services, information may also be processed according to the privacy practices of the relevant provider.

10. Sharing of Information

Nanobricks does not intend to sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers, professional advisers or other parties when reasonably necessary to operate the business, provide requested services or comply with applicable obligations.

11. Data Retention

Nanobricks may retain enquiry, customer, training and business communication information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, business record keeping or applicable requirements.

Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

12. Your Requests

If you have submitted personal information to Nanobricks, you may contact us regarding questions, corrections or reasonable requests relating to that information.

We may need to verify the identity of a requester before acting on certain requests.

13. Children's Information

Nanobricks Academy may offer educational programs involving students. Where participation by minors is permitted, appropriate information or consent from a parent, guardian, institution or authorized representative may be requested where required.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Nanobricks may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, systems or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page.

This Privacy Policy provides general information about the current website and business practices of Nanobricks. Individual customer, training or commercial engagements may be subject to additional agreements, notices or terms.
