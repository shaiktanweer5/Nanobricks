Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Managed IT / AMC service?

It provides ongoing technical support and maintenance for an organization's agreed IT environment under a structured service arrangement.

Do you support small businesses?

Yes. Our services can be customized for smaller offices, startups and growing businesses.

Do you provide onsite support?

Yes. Scheduled onsite support can be included depending on the selected package and location.

Can you support cloud environments?

Yes. Nanobricks supports AWS, Azure and Google Cloud operational requirements depending on the agreed scope.

Do you provide 24x7 support?

Standard packages are based on agreed business support hours. Extended or after-hours support can be discussed separately based on requirements.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on users, devices, servers, cloud accounts, locations, onsite requirements and overall support scope.
